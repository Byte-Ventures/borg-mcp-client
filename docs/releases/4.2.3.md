# borgmcp 4.2.3

- The Codex wake-path health surface now reflects real delivery state. Previously `borg_stream-status` and `borg_regen` reported the Codex wake path as healthy whenever the app-server bridge was armed, even while a directed activity-log entry sat undelivered — a wake deferred behind a mid-turn thread, retrying, or unable to resolve a target could read healthy for many minutes. The health now folds in the live delivery state, so a deferred, retrying, failed, or undeliverable wake reports degraded instead, and `borg_stream-status` surfaces the selected target, the last injection attempt and result, the deferred-queue state, and the last failure code (no message contents or secrets). Health returns to healthy as soon as the wake is delivered or the unread log is drained by any path. The wake mechanism, retry cadence, and timing are unchanged, and delivery remains exactly-once.
