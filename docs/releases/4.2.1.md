# borgmcp 4.2.1

- `borg_read-log` structured results are now proportional to the entries they return. The structured payload carries exactly `entries`, `behind_by`, and `has_more` — a wake drain no longer ships the full cube roster alongside them (~54KB per drain, even empty, down to under 1KB for small and empty drains). Entries keep their `drone_label` and `role_name` fields, `borg_roster` remains the identity-mapping surface, and the human-readable text rendering is unchanged.
