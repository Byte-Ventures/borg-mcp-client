# borgmcp 4.1.0

- Launches the human-seat drone last during `borg quickstart`, after every non-human drone in their existing relative order. The launch priority follows persisted human-seat metadata rather than a role name; rosters without a human seat and ordinary `borg launch-all` keep their existing order. This provides a last-created-window signal, but terminal applications and window managers may choose a different focused window.
